# Wodi Explains: What is Forge Studio?

Script for an AI-generated or recorded explainer video. Use this with any video AI (e.g. Sora, Runway, HeyGen, or a simple avatar + TTS) to produce a short “What is Forge Studio?” clip. Wodi is the mascot: a friendly, Toy Story–style character that represents the VForge agent and Forge Studio.

---

## Character: Wodi

- **Role:** Guide and sidekick for Forge Studio and the VForge agent.
- **Vibe:** Approachable, a bit playful, helpful—like a small companion from a Toy Story–style world.
- **Visual idea:** Cute, simple character (e.g. bear or bot) that can sit in the corner of the UI or appear in the video to explain features and suggest Bear components.

**Scene 3 – CTA**  
*(Screen: “Forge Studio – Coming soon” and link to portal.)*

> Try Forge Studio when it’s ready. Until then, explore Bear and the rest of the Forge stack on the portal.
